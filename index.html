Frequently asked questions

Is Bolo really free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no sign-up.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the page, tap the mic and speak.

Which languages are supported?

All languages in the list, depending on your browser. Bhojpuri and Maithili are not offered by browsers yet, so use Hindi for them.

Is my data safe?

Text and clips stay on your device. Audio is processed by your browser's speech service, so an internet connection is needed. The Correct with AI button sends your text to an AI service, and only when you tap it.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. It works in Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone. Firefox does not support speech recognition.
